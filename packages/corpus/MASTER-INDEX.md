# Master Metric Index

Every metric surfaced by the breadth pass, by family. This is the scoping list: what becomes a six-facet card in v1.

Source research lives alongside this file in `research/`. Two supplements carry primary-source depth that did not fit a single family: `03a` (bookings, ACV, TCV, built on SEC filings and staff comment letters) and `03b` (the quick ratio name collision).
